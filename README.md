📊 Sales Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive, multi-metric Power BI dashboard built to monitor sales performance across categories, regions, and time periods — with a focus on identifying profit-margin risks alongside revenue growth.

🔑 Key Metrics

Number of Orders: 128

🛠️ Tools & Tech
Power BI Desktop — dashboard design & data modeling
DAX — calculated KPIs and measures
Power Query — data cleaning and transformation
📈 What the Dashboard Covers

1. KPI Overview At-a-glance cards for Total Sales, Orders, Avg. Order Value, Total Profit, and Profit % — giving a single-view snapshot of business health.

2. Monthly Sales Trend Line chart tracking sales fluctuation across Jan 2014–Jan 2015, highlighting peak and low-performing months.

3. Profit by Season Quarterly profit trend (Qtr 1–Qtr 4) to surface seasonal performance patterns.

4. Sales by Segment & Category Breakdown of sales across customer segments (Corporate, Consumer, Home Office) and product categories (Furniture, Office Supplies, Technology).

5. Profit vs. Discount Analysis Scatter plot examining the relationship between discount levels and average profit — used to flag discount strategies that erode margins.

6. Ship Mode Popularity Bar chart comparing sales volume across shipping methods (Standard, Second, First, Same Day Class).

7. Products With High Sales but Low Profit A detailed table surfacing products that generate strong revenue but weak or negative profit — critical for pricing/margin decisions.

🎯 Business Value

This dashboard enables stakeholders to move beyond raw sales totals and identify where margin is being lost — through discount overuse, low-profit high-volume products, or inefficient shipping choices — supporting more profitable, data-driven decision-making.
